ABOUT ME

A developer who cares how it feels, not just how it looks

I'm Adeleye R. Oluwafemi, a frontend web developer helping small businesses, creators and startups show up online with confidence.

My story

I began with curiosity: taking apart websites I admired to understand why they worked. That curiosity turned into a craft. Today I build clean, hand-coded frontends where every element has a job.

My focus is the part of a website that people actually touch—layout, typography, speed and interaction. I care about pages that stay readable on a small phone with a weak connection, because that's how most people will meet your brand.

Alongside development I edit short-form video in CapCut, which means I can help with both the website and the content that drives traffic to it.
